# Curriculum: Investment Appraisal
**Prof. Dr. Christian Kraus | THWS | Bachelor International Business**

**Target Audience:** International bachelor students, no prior finance background required.  
**Goal:** Build intuition and calculation competence for time value of money and investment decision-making.

---

## Part I: Interest Calculations (Chapters 1–4)

Foundation block. Establishes the mathematical and conceptual toolkit before any investment decision can be made.

| # | Title | Core Concepts |
|---|-------|---------------|
| 1 | The Time Machine | Time value of money, opportunity cost, simple vs. compound interest, discounting / compounding |
| 2 | The Geometry of Cash Flows | Annuities (ordinary / due), PVIFA, Capital Recovery Factor, perpetuities, Gordon Growth Model |
| 3 | Killing the Debt | Annuity loan, constant-amortization loan, bullet loan, supplier credit (Skonto), Dean Model intro |
| 4 | Valuation | Bond valuation (price, yield), stock valuation (preferred stock, common stock / Gordon Growth, P/E ratio) |

---

## Part II: Investment Appraisal (Chapters 5–9)

Application block. Uses the Part I toolkit to evaluate real investment decisions.

| # | Title | Core Concepts |
|---|-------|---------------|
| 5 | The North Star – NPV | Fisher Separation Theorem, Net Present Value formula, decision rule, reinvestment assumption, capital value function, scale problem |
| 6 | The Percentage Trap – IRR | IRR definition & hurdle rate, iteration + Regula Falsi, reinvestment critique, Modified IRR (Baldwin Rate), Descartes' rule, NPV vs. IRR showdown |
| 7 | Periodizing Success – Annuity Method | Converting NPV to periodic income, comparing projects with unequal lifespans |
| 8 | The Silent Partner – Taxes | Tax-adjusted cash flows, depreciation tax shield, after-tax discount rate, tax paradox |
| 9 | The Master Plan – Dean Model | Investment demand/supply curves, endogenous cut-off rate, capital rationing, Profitability Index |

---

## Learning Arc

```
Time Value of Money
    → Cash Flow Patterns (Annuities)
        → Debt Instruments & Valuation
            → NPV as primary decision criterion
                → IRR, Annuity Method as alternatives/supplements
                    → Tax effects & capital constraints
```

## Key Competencies on Exit

- Calculate present and future values under simple and compound interest
- Price bonds and stocks using discounted cash flow models
- Build and read amortization schedules for the three standard loan types
- Apply NPV, IRR, and Annuity Method to evaluate and rank investment projects
- Adjust appraisal for corporate taxes (tax shield, tax paradox)
- Allocate capital rationally under budget constraints (Dean Model / Profitability Index)
